## Team Values

* Open-mindedness 	  
  * Allows everyone to feel heard and welcome   
  * Bolster idea generation because everyone feels comfortable sharing  
  * Be ready to compromise\!\!\!  
  * Listen to everyone’s ideas\!  
* Responsibility   
  * Make clear designations of responsibilities, but also leave room for individuals to express a need for help if their portion is taking longer or is more complicated than expected  
  * If someone is struggling with their responsibility, communicate early and often so that roadbumps are addressed quickly   
  * Everyone should do their work on time so that nothing has to get done super close to the deadline  
  * Everyone should try their best on the work they do, and make sure the code is commented so everyone on the team can read and understand the code  
* Togetherness  
  * Prioritizing team trust and cohesion above all   
  * Lean on each other when needed  
  * Caring about the outcome of the project and not just your portion  
  * Make sure to communicate and try to resolve issues within the group before escalating them

## GitHub Expectations

* Use GitHub issues to track all progress and assign specific members tasks  
* Use pull requests and get them reviewed by someone before adding to the codebase    
* Be descriptive in commit messages to allow other team members to stay informed

## Team Communication

* Express any concerns early on to continuously improve as a team & not repeat mistakes. Roadblocks should be communicated to the whole team as soon as they occur  
* Be honest and open about your skill sets so we can best allocate work, and put in the effort to learn new skills as needed  
* Developers are expected to meet on Tuesdays and Thursdays, and once outside of class time each week  
  * At these meetings, developers will share progress updates, roadblocks, and plans  
  * Team members are expected to participate in all meetings unless ill or dealing with an emergency. Other absences must be communicated 24 hours in advance.  
  * Be on time for meetings :)  
  * If you have to miss a meeting, be proactive in catching up to speed by reviewing meeting notes or speaking with other team members  
* Slack will be the primary communication tool for project-related matters  
  * Engage in a conversation in a dedicated thread (click "Reply to thread") for questions, comments, objections, etc.  
  * Use the 👍 emoji to indicate agreement or acknowledgment  
  * Use the ✅ emoji to show that action has been taken in response to a thread  
  * Use the 👀 emoji to indicate that you will look into the matter later  
  * Responses are expected within 6 hours from 9 am-6 pm on weekdays; otherwise, a 24-hour response is expected.
